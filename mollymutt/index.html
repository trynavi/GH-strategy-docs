01

Increase Conversion of i don't like mondays dog bed duvet

Most pet parents don't understand the complete stuff sack system on first visit. Education at the right moment converts browsers into believers in the mollymutt way.

📏
Critical Impact

Build a bed-sizing quiz to eliminate duvet-stuffing confusion

With duvets at $40-60 requiring specific stuff sacks, create a 3-question quiz (dog size, existing pillow dimensions, sleep style) that recommends the exact duvet + stuff combo. This removes the #1 purchase hesitation and increases AOV by bundling from the start.

🛒
Critical Impact

Deploy abandoned cart flows with sustainability storytelling

For a $40-60 duvet purchase, trigger a 3-email sequence at 1hr/24hr/72hr highlighting: (1) the landfill diversion impact of their specific cart, (2) customer photos of completed beds with similar duvets, (3) a "complete the bed" discount if they add a stuff sack. Lean into the eco-mission that differentiates mollymutt.

⭐
High Impact

Feature user-generated bed photos in welcome flow emails

New subscribers see beautiful duvets but may not visualize the full stuffed bed. Send 2-3 pre-purchase emails showcasing real customer setups with various patterns, linking directly to "shop this look" product bundles. Social proof removes the "will this actually work?" barrier.

02

Increase Repurchase of i don't like mondays dog bed duvet

Dog beds get dirty. Position duvet repurchase as seasonal refreshing, not replacement—making it easy and expected for customers to rotate patterns as part of responsible pet parenting.

🧺
Critical Impact

Launch a "Refresh Your Bed" email at 6-month post-purchase mark

After 6 months of washing and daily use, duvets show wear. Trigger an automated email with subject line "Time for a Fresh Look?" featuring new seasonal patterns, emphasizing mollymutt's machine-washable durability while suggesting it's smart to rotate duvets. Include a 15% "refresh discount" to incentivize the second duvet purchase.

📬
High Impact

Send a care postcard with pattern-mixing inspiration at 30 days

Mail a physical postcard showing how customers layer duvets seasonally (lighter summer patterns, cozier winter ones) with washing instructions and a QR code to "build your duvet wardrobe." Direct mail at $1.50 per piece cuts through digital noise and positions multiple duvets as aspirational, not excessive.

🎨
High Impact

Create a "Pattern Match" replenishment series tied to home decor seasons

Segment by duvet pattern purchased and send quarterly emails (spring, summer, fall, winter) showcasing complementary seasonal patterns. Example: "i don't like mondays" buyers get shown lighter florals for spring refreshes. Position it as "redecorating your pup's space" rather than product replacement.

03

Increase Cross-Sell to Other Products

Duvet buyers need stuff sacks, crate mats, and amarillo blankets to complete the system. Strategic product education expands customers from single-item to full mollymutt ecosystems.

📦
Critical Impact

Build post-purchase flows that complete the bed system

If someone buys just a duvet (not the stuff sack), trigger Day 7 and Day 14 emails explaining what to stuff it with—highlighting the $60-85 stuff sacks and showing the environmental impact of repurposing old blankets. Capture the 40-60% of customers who buy piecemeal and convert them to complete systems.

🚗
High Impact

Introduce crate mats and travel gear in a "Beyond the Bed" campaign

45 days post-duvet purchase, send a targeted email to customers highlighting crate pads ($32-42) and amarillo blankets ($30) as "everywhere else your pup sleeps" solutions. Use customer stories of dogs who love their mollymutt beds at home now wanting comfort in the car or crate. Positions cross-sell as solving a new problem.

🎁
Medium Impact

Create "Gift for the Dog Parent" segmented campaigns

Use RFM analysis to identify high-value customers (2+ purchases, $120+ LTV) and send them monthly "complete your collection" offers featuring complementary patterns and products. Position additional duvets as gifts for multi-dog households or backup sets, increasing basket size from $50 to $120+.

✨ Quick Wins

Additional Retention Levers

Three high-impact operational improvements to strengthen mollymutt's customer foundation.

💬

Communication: Add SMS to capture high-intent moments

Launch SMS with a 10% discount incentive at checkout, then use it exclusively for back-in-stock alerts (patterns sell out), flash sales, and 2-hour abandoned cart reminders. Pet parents are mobile-first and will respond to timely product drops.

📸

Trust & Community: Launch a photo contest for bed setups

Run a quarterly "#MollyMuttMonday" Instagram contest where customers share their styled dog beds. Feature winners in email, on PDPs, and create a "Community Beds" gallery page. UGC solves the visualization problem and builds brand love.

🔄

Customer Experience: Offer a "Love It or Exchange It" pattern guarantee

Remove pattern purchase anxiety with a 30-day exchange policy (pattern for pattern, not refund). Communicate this heavily on PDPs and in welcome emails. Reduces returns while giving hesitant buyers confidence to try bolder designs.
