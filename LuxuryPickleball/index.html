01

Increase Conversion of Titan ACE Pickleball Machine

At $2,999, the Titan ACE is a significant investment. Pre-purchase education and social proof are essential to converting serious players evaluating premium training solutions.

🎯
Critical Impact

Build a Titan ACE-specific abandonment flow with ROI justification

Create a 3-email sequence for cart abandoners that addresses the $2,999 price point head-on—calculate cost per training session vs. coaching fees, highlight the programmable drills and oscillation features that replace a practice partner, and include video testimonials from club players who've improved their game.

📹
Critical Impact

Integrate UGC and setup videos in your welcome flow

For browsers who haven't purchased yet, create a nurture series showcasing real customers using the Titan ACE in their driveways and courts. Feature the remote control functionality, battery life (4-8 hours), and ball capacity (135 balls) to overcome common objections about complexity and portability.

💬
High Impact

Survey new subscribers to segment by skill level and training goals

Ask prospects about their current skill level (beginner, intermediate, advanced), primary training goal (consistency, power, placement), and practice environment (home driveway, club court). Use this to personalize messaging around which Titan ACE features (random oscillation, spin control, customizable speeds) solve their specific needs.

02

Increase Repurchase of Titan ACE Pickleball Machine

While the Titan ACE is a one-time purchase, driving consumable replenishment (balls) and complementary equipment is critical to maximizing customer lifetime value beyond the initial $2,999 sale.

📚
Critical Impact

Launch a post-purchase training drill library delivered via email

Send Titan ACE buyers a 6-week drip series with progressive training programs—start with beginner drills (consistent feeds for forehand practice), advance to intermediate (random oscillation for footwork), and finish with tournament prep sequences. Include embedded videos showing exact machine settings and court positioning to drive engagement and reduce buyer's remorse.

📦
High Impact

Create a ball replenishment reminder at the 90-day mark

Heavy users will wear through pickleballs quickly with 135-ball capacity drills. Trigger an automated email 90 days post-purchase offering bulk pickleball packs and highlighting that worn balls reduce machine accuracy. Include a "Titan ACE owner" discount code to reinforce their premium status and drive consumable revenue.

🎁
High Impact

Send a personalized direct mail piece at 6 months with upgrade accessories

Mail Titan ACE owners a premium postcard featuring the protective cover, replacement remote, or extended battery options with their machine serial number referenced. This tactile touchpoint reinforces the premium positioning and drives accessory revenue while they're fully engaged with the product.

03

Increase Cross-Sell to Other Products

Titan ACE buyers are premium customers who've invested in serious skill development—they're prime candidates for high-end paddles, court equipment, and training accessories.

🏓
Critical Impact

Build paddle upgrade journeys based on the drills they're practicing

Segment Titan ACE owners by the training content they engage with—those watching power drill videos get targeted with your GRUVN paddles emphasizing spin and power, while those focused on control drills see the Vanguard Power Air Invikta. Send these recommendations 45 days post-purchase when they're seeing improvement and attributing it to practice quality.

🎯
High Impact

Create "Complete Your Training Setup" bundles in post-purchase flows

30 days after Titan ACE delivery, send a curated bundle email featuring your portable nets, ball hoppers, and premium pickleballs as the natural next step. Position it as "what serious players add to maximize their Titan ACE sessions"—increasing both AOV and purchase frequency from your highest-value segment.

📊
Medium Impact

Implement RFM segmentation for your premium paddle launches

When you release new premium paddles like the Vanguard or GRUVN models, prioritize Titan ACE owners in your launch communications—they're your most engaged, highest-spending customers. Give them early access or exclusive colorways to reinforce their status and drive cross-category purchases at premium price points.

Additional Retention Levers

Trust & Community: Implement a structured review collection campaign for the Titan ACE

At $2,999, your highest-ticket item needs extensive social proof. Identify which traffic sources bring customers who buy once (Titan ACE) vs. those who return for paddles, balls, and accessories. Double down on channels driving cross-category buyers, and create specific nurture tracks for one-time machine buyers to increase their purchase frequency.
