Increase Repurchase of Testosterone Cypionate Injections

TRT is a long-term commitment. Bridge the expectation gap between starting treatment and feeling results by educating patients on timelines, managing subscription changes, and reinforcing the value of continuity.

📆
Critical Impact

Launch a Post-Injection Timeline Education Flow

After first injection shipment, send a 6-week email series tied to the TRT results timeline: Week 1 (what to expect), Week 3 (early energy shifts), Week 6 (libido/mood improvements), Week 12 (body composition changes). Set realistic expectations to reduce churn during the "patience phase" before noticeable results.

🔔
Critical Impact

Implement Subscription Pause Prevention for Active Subscribers

When patients attempt to pause or cancel their $199/month subscription, trigger an intervention flow offering flexible dosing adjustments, a check-in call with their physician, or a one-month discount to stay enrolled. TRT requires consistency—one skipped month can reset progress and lead to permanent churn.

Medium Impact

Send Direct Mail at the 90-Day Milestone

At 90 days (when most men start seeing significant results), mail a personalized "Your Progress" card celebrating their commitment to optimization. Include a blood work reminder and a handwritten note from their care team. Physical touchpoints build emotional loyalty in a digital-first category.

30 days after starting TRT injections, introduce fertility-preserving options like HCG or Enclomiphene via email. Position these as "stack" add-ons for men concerned about testicular atrophy or future family planning. Bundle pricing incentive: Add HCG for $75/month (vs. $99 standalone).

High Impact

Promote Blood Work Monitoring at Strategic Treatment Intervals

At months 3, 6, and 12, send automated reminders to order comprehensive blood panels ($150). Frame it as treatment optimization—not just compliance. Include before/after hormone level visualizations from other patients to show the value of data-driven adjustments.

Analyze purchase frequency and treatment adherence to create three tiers: Core (TRT only), Optimizer (TRT + 1 add-on), Elite (TRT + multiple therapies).
