Increase Repurchase of Dead Sea Facial & Body Mud Mask

Mineral-based skincare requires consistent use for best results. Bridge the expectation gap between purchase and visible transformation to drive timely replenishment.

📅
Critical Impact

Launch post-purchase education flow with usage timeline and results expectations

Send a 4-email series teaching optimal application (2-3x weekly), setting expectations for when results appear (week 1: deep clean feeling, week 2-3: reduced pores, week 4+: texture improvement), and reinforcing the mineral science. This keeps engagement high during the crucial 60-90 day replenishment window.

⏰
Critical Impact

Trigger replenishment reminder at 75 days based on typical consumption rate

Your 8.8oz jar lasts approximately 2-3 months with recommended use. Set up automated email/SMS at day 75 post-purchase offering "ready for a refill?" messaging with easy reorder button and consideration for a multi-jar discount (buy 2, save 15%) to extend time between orders.

💌
High Impact

Deploy direct mail postcard at 90 days featuring customer transformation stories

For one-time buyers who haven't repurchased by day 90, send a physical postcard showcasing 2-3 dramatic before/after customer photos with the headline "Your best skin is worth another jar." Include a unique 20% discount code to drive the second purchase and begin building routine.

03

Increase Cross-Sell to Other Products

Your catalog includes serums, eye creams, and body care that complement the mud mask perfectly. Strategic sequencing turns single-product buyers into full-routine customers.

🎁
High Impact

Create post-purchase bundles pairing mud mask with Dead Sea Mineral Facial Serum

At day 14 post-purchase (when they're seeing results), email mud mask buyers offering your Vitamin C Serum or Hydrating Facial Serum at a "complete your routine" discount. Position as the perfect follow-up to maximize mineral absorption and maintain the glow between mask applications.

📊
High Impact

Segment campaigns by purchase frequency—VIPs get early access to new launches

Use RFM segmentation to identify your top 20% of customers (2+ purchases, recent activity). Give this group exclusive 48-hour early access to new products like your body scrubs or specialty masks, creating FOMO and rewarding loyalty without a formal points program.

🔄
Medium Impact

Build seasonal cross-sell journeys promoting body products in summer, face care in winter

Leverage natural buying behavior—promote your Dead Sea Body Butter and body mud treatments May-August when customers think about exposed skin, then shift to intensive facial masks and eye creams September-February when focus returns to face. Match messaging to seasonal concerns for higher relevance.
