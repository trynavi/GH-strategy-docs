01

Increase Conversion of TPC Sawgrass

Turn browsing golfers into buyers by showcasing the premium quality and nostalgic connection to iconic courses they've played or dream of playing.

🎬
Critical Impact

Build a Welcome Series Showcasing the Craft Behind the Map

At $59, TPC Sawgrass is a considered purchase. Launch a 3-email welcome flow showing the hand-illustrated design process, frame quality details, and customer photos in home offices and man caves. Include a limited-time 10% discount to convert browsers who land via social or paid ads.

🏌️
Critical Impact

Deploy Browse Abandonment Flows with Course-Specific Nostalgia

When someone views TPC Sawgrass but doesn't buy, trigger an email within 2 hours featuring UGC from golfers who've played the 17th island green, plus testimonials about gifting this map. Remind them of the free shipping threshold and suggest pairing with another TPC course for collectors.

⭐
High Impact

Integrate Customer Reviews and In-Situ Photos on Product Pages

Golfers want to see how the 24x18 size looks framed on a real wall. Add a review carousel directly below the product images showing customer installations, mention of quality packaging, and testimonials from gifts received. This visual proof reduces hesitation on sizing and quality at this premium price point.

02

Increase Repurchase of TPC Sawgrass

Turn one-time buyers into collectors by nurturing their passion for golf art and creating natural moments to complete their wall gallery or gift to friends.

📬
Critical Impact

Send Post-Purchase "Complete Your Collection" Direct Mail at 30 Days

When TPC Sawgrass arrives and gets framed, send a high-quality postcard at 30 days featuring your top 4 courses (Pebble Beach, Augusta, St. Andrews, Bethpage Black) with a personalized "Start Your Wall of Fame" collector's discount. Physical mail stands out and triggers an emotional buying moment when they're already loving their first map.

🎁
High Impact

Trigger Gift Occasion Flows at Key Calendar Windows

TPC Sawgrass buyers are ideal gifters. Deploy automated campaigns 3 weeks before Father's Day, Christmas, and golf buddy birthdays (ask for dates in post-purchase survey) reminding them their friends would love a course map. Include gift messaging and offer discounted 2-pack bundles to make gifting frictionless.

🔄
High Impact

Launch a 90-Day Win-Back Series for One-Time Buyers

Buyers who loved TPC Sawgrass but haven't returned in 90 days get a 3-touch reactivation: Email 1 shows new course releases, Email 2 features customer gallery walls with 3+ maps, Email 3 offers a "complete the set" discount on complementary Florida courses (Bay Hill, Copperhead). Position collecting as a journey, not a transaction.

03

Increase Cross-Sell to Other Products

Maximize lifetime value by guiding TPC Sawgrass buyers to complementary courses that match their golf history and aspirations.

🗺️
Critical Impact

Build Post-Purchase Flows Based on Course Affinity Segments

Tag TPC Sawgrass buyers by geography and behavior. Florida residents get recommendations for Streamsong and Doral within 7 days. Travelers who likely played TPC during a Ponte Vedra trip receive Scottish links courses (St. Andrews, Carnoustie) at day 14. West coasters see Pebble Beach and Torrey Pines. Personalized suggestions drive 2nd purchases 3x higher than generic cross-sells.

📦
High Impact

Create Curated "Bucket List" Bundles at Checkout and in Campaigns

Package TPC Sawgrass with Augusta National, Pebble Beach, and St. Andrews as "The Major Championship Collection" bundle at $199 (vs. $236 individually). Promote this bundle in abandoned cart flows and post-purchase emails. Collectors want themes—bundle by PGA Tour stops, major venues, or geographic regions to increase AOV from $59 to $199+.

📊
Medium Impact

Segment Campaigns by RFM Scores to Match Product Depth

Use RFM segmentation to identify your top 20% collectors (3+ purchases, recent activity). Send them exclusive early access to new course releases like Kiawah Ocean or Whistling Straits with VIP pricing. Meanwhile, 1-time buyers get general catalog reminders. Tailored intensity prevents fatigue while maximizing revenue from high-value collectors who want every map.

✨ Quick Wins

Additional Retention Levers

Three high-impact operational improvements to strengthen CourseMaps' customer foundation.

💬

Trust & Community: Launch a Post-Purchase Photo Survey

Email buyers 2 weeks after delivery asking them to share a photo of their framed map with the subject line "Show Us Your Setup." Offer a $10 credit toward their next map. This generates authentic UGC for product pages and social proof while gathering testimonials that convert browsers into buyers.

📲

Communication Channels: Add SMS at Checkout for Shipping Updates

Golf fans are mobile-first. Capture SMS opt-ins at checkout with "Get tracking updates via text" positioning. Use this list for shipment notifications, then transition to abandoned browse texts and new course launch alerts. SMS drives 8x higher engagement than email for time-sensitive promotions.

📈

Data & Tech: Track Repeat Purchase Rate by First Course Purchased

Build a dashboard showing repeat rate by which course was bought first. If TPC Sawgrass buyers repeat at 18% but Pebble Beach buyers repeat at 32%, you'll know which hero products to prioritize in acquisition. Use this data to optimize ad creative and landing pages toward highest-LTV entry products.
