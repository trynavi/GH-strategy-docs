01

Increase Conversion of Rush Hour Serum

Optimize the first-touch experience for this $68 active serum by addressing skin concerns, building trust through education, and making subscription the obvious choice.

📋
Critical Impact

Segment new subscribers by primary skin concern during welcome flow

Since Rush Hour Serum targets multiple benefits (brightening, barrier repair, texture), ask subscribers upfront what they're solving for. Tailor subsequent welcome emails to emphasize the specific actives (tranexamic acid for dark spots, peptides for texture) that match their goal, increasing product-benefit alignment and first purchase intent.

💳
Critical Impact

Optimize subscription sign-up with first-order savings incentive

At $68 per bottle, Rush Hour Serum is a premium commitment. Test offering 15% off first subscription orders (bringing it to $57.80) vs. free shipping, clearly stating the 30-45 day usage window on PDPs and cart. Since subscription buyers have 3-5x higher LTV, this discount pays for itself in retention alone.

🛒
High Impact

Build Rush Hour Serum cart abandonment flow with ingredient education

Create a 3-email sequence specifically for Rush Hour cart abandoners: (1) ingredient spotlight on tranexamic acid + 5% niacinamide within 1 hour, (2) before/after results timeline at 12 hours, (3) testimonials from similar skin types at 24 hours. Beauty buyers need education to justify premium pricing—make it automatic.

02

Increase Repurchase of Rush Hour Serum

Bridge the expectation gap between purchase and visible results by educating on the science, timing usage perfectly, and reducing subscription churn with proactive engagement.

📚
Critical Impact

Launch post-purchase education flow with results timeline anchoring

Most active serums require 4-8 weeks for visible results. Create a 6-email post-purchase journey: Day 3 (application tips for max absorption), Day 7 (what to expect week 1), Day 14 (texture improvements begin), Day 28 (checkpoint + photo prompt), Day 35 (brightening typically visible), Day 40 (replenishment reminder). Set realistic expectations to prevent premature churn.

⏰
Critical Impact

Reduce subscription churn with proactive pause/skip optimization

For customers approaching their subscription renewal, send a "check-in" email 5 days before processing asking if they need to pause, skip, or adjust frequency. Offering control before they cancel reduces churn by 20-30%. Include usage tips if they're going through bottles too slowly (applying too much product) or suggest gifting if overstock builds.

📬
High Impact

Send direct mail at Day 30 with usage optimization guide

At the critical one-month mark (when Rush Hour Serum is ~50% depleted), mail a high-quality postcard with layering tips, AM/PM routine optimization, and a QR code to results stories. Physical mail has 80%+ open rates and signals premium brand investment. This touchpoint reinforces commitment right when results are beginning to show.

03

Increase Cross-Sell to Other Products

Maximize customer lifetime value by strategically introducing complementary products from Off We Glow's catalog at high-intent moments in the skincare journey.

🧪
High Impact

Build post-purchase routine completion flow for Daydream Moisturizer

Rush Hour Serum buyers need a moisturizer to seal in actives. At Day 10 (after they're in a rhythm), send an educational email explaining why pairing with Daydream Moisturizer optimizes tranexamic acid absorption and prevents irritation. Include a one-time 20% bundle discount code. Routine completion cross-sells convert 30-40% higher than generic recommendations.

📊
High Impact

Segment RFM data to promote SPF products to high-value customers

Customers who've purchased Rush Hour Serum 2+ times (high frequency) are educated on actives and likely ready for complementary products. Target this segment with campaigns for Off We Glow's SPF offerings (essential when using brightening actives). Their product knowledge and demonstrated spend make them 3x more likely to convert on premium add-ons.

🎁
Medium Impact

Create value bundles pairing Rush Hour Serum with cleansers

Analyze purchase path data to identify which products Rush Hour buyers purchase next. If cleansers rank high, create a "Complete Clarity Set" bundle (Rush Hour Serum + cleanser + moisturizer) at 15% off individual pricing. Promote this during subscription renewal windows and in cart upsells to increase AOV and introduce product breadth early in the relationship.

Additional Retention Levers

Three high-impact operational improvements to strengthen Off We Glow's customer foundation.

⭐

Trust & Community: Implement post-purchase review collection at Day 35

Rush Hour Serum results peak around week 5. Automate review requests via email/SMS at Day 35 when customers are most enthusiastic. Include a photo upload incentive (10% off next order). UGC from real results increases conversion rates by 25-35% and provides social proof for your premium $68 price point.

📱

Communication: Add SMS to high-intent moments in retention flows

Email is strong for education, but SMS drives immediate action. Add SMS to: subscription renewal reminders (5 days before), cart abandonment (3 hours after), and restocks of sold-out products. SMS open rates of 95%+ ensure time-sensitive messages land when they matter most.

🎯

Data & Tech: Track repurchase rate by acquisition channel

Not all customers are created equal. Measure which channels (paid social, influencer, organic) drive the highest 60-day repurchase rates for Rush Hour Serum. Double down on retention-focused channels and adjust messaging for low-retention sources. This single metric can shift CAC efficiency by 40%+.
