01

Increase Conversion of Embr Wave 2

At $349, the Wave 2 is a considered purchase. Bridge the education gap between awareness and understanding so buyers feel confident investing in thermal wellness.

🧪
Critical Impact

Segment welcome subscribers by thermal wellness goal (sleep, stress, menopause, focus)

Send a one-question survey immediately after email opt-in asking what they're hoping to solve. This lets you tailor the welcome series with relevant testimonials and use cases—like highlighting the "Fall Asleep" session for insomnia sufferers or "Calm" for anxiety management.

🛡️
High Impact

Deploy an abandonment flow that addresses the two biggest objections: "Does it actually work?" and "Is it worth $349?"

In email 1, embed a 60-second video testimonial from a verified customer showing their daily Wave 2 routine. In email 2, break down the cost vs. alternatives ($349 one-time vs. $30/month sleep supplements = ROI in 12 months) and emphasize the 30-day money-back guarantee.

📲
High Impact

Optimize the subscription messaging on the Wave 2 product page to highlight Premium app features

Most buyers won't realize that Wave 2 Premium unlocks additional thermal sessions, sleep tracking, and personalized recommendations. Add a side-by-side comparison module showing Free vs. Premium benefits, and test offering the first month free with device purchase to drive attachment rate.

02

Increase Repurchase of Embr Wave 2

Wave 2 is a durable device, not a consumable. Retention here means subscription renewals, gifting, and cross-sells to accessories. Keep users engaged so the device becomes indispensable.

📚
Critical Impact

Build a 90-day post-purchase education journey to drive habit formation and Premium subscription retention

Day 7: Send usage tips ("Try the Fall Asleep session 20 minutes before bed"). Day 21: Share data on habit formation ("Most users feel a difference within 3 weeks"). Day 60: Feature a customer story. Day 90: Offer a Premium subscription renewal incentive for lapsed trial users.

💳
Critical Impact

Reduce subscription churn with pre-dunning campaigns and failed payment recovery flows

Many Premium subscribers churn passively due to expired cards. Set up an automated email 5 days before billing that reminds them to update payment info, emphasizes Premium benefits, and includes a one-click update link. For failed payments, follow up within 24 hours with SMS and email.

🎁
High Impact

Launch a "Gift Another Wave" campaign targeting satisfied customers 4-6 months post-purchase

Wave 2 is ideal for gifting—especially for menopause support, sleep struggles, or chronic stress. Send an email with subject line "Know someone who needs this?" offering $50 off a second Wave 2. Include a personal note feature so buyers can explain why they're gifting thermal wellness.

03

Increase Cross-Sell to Other Products

Maximize customer lifetime value by introducing complementary products and accessories that enhance the Wave 2 experience and keep customers in the Embr ecosystem.

⌚
High Impact

Create a post-purchase flow introducing replacement bands and charging accessories at strategic intervals

At 6 months, send an email showcasing the Sport Band ($39) or Leather Band ($79) as a refresh option—"Upgrade your Wave 2 style." At 12 months, feature the charging cable replacement. Time these with seasonal gifting windows (holidays, birthdays) for max relevance.

🏆
High Impact

Launch a loyalty program that rewards repeat purchases and app engagement with exclusive perks

Offer points for Premium subscription renewals, referrals, and reviews. Let customers redeem points for accessories, app content unlocks, or early access to new thermal sessions. This creates stickiness beyond the device itself and encourages cross-category exploration.

📦
Medium Impact

Build product bundles that pair Wave 2 with accessories for first-time buyers and gifters

Test bundles like "Wave 2 Complete" (device + extra band + 3 months Premium for $399) or "Sleep Starter Kit" (Wave 2 + sleep tracking insights). Display these prominently on the product page and in cart abandonment emails to increase AOV and reduce decision fatigue.

✨ Quick Wins

Additional Retention Levers

Three high-impact operational improvements to strengthen EmbrLabs' customer foundation.

📱

Communication: Add SMS to your highest-performing email automations

Start with cart abandonment and post-purchase check-ins. SMS has 5x higher open rates than email for time-sensitive messages. Test a 2-touch sequence: email at 2 hours, SMS at 24 hours for cart abandoners.

⭐

Trust & Community: Feature video testimonials prominently across site and flows

Your 1,800+ reviews are powerful social proof, but video testimonials showing real people using Wave 2 in their daily routines will convert skeptics faster. Collect via post-purchase email incentive ($25 gift card) and embed on PDP, homepage, and welcome series.

📊

Data & Tech: Implement cohort retention tracking by acquisition channel

Not all Wave 2 buyers are equal. Track 30/60/90-day retention by channel (paid social, organic, influencer, etc.) to identify which sources drive the stickiest customers. Double down on high-LTV channels and adjust messaging for lower-performing ones.
