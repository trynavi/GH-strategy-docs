01

Increase Conversion of Original Chair

Turn browsers into buyers by showing how the $59.95 Original Chair solves their specific outdoor seating problems—whether it's kayaking, camping, or stadium events.

🎯
Critical Impact

Build an abandonment flow showcasing the Original Chair's versatility

Create a 3-email sequence that targets cart abandoners with specific use cases (kayaking back support, stadium seating, campsite comfort) and customer photos showing the chair in action. Include the 40-year legacy story to overcome price objections at $59.95.

📸
High Impact

Integrate user-generated content throughout the pre-purchase journey

Leverage the adventure-focused lifestyle imagery you already have and add customer testimonials showing real use cases. Create a welcome series that features customers using the Original Chair for kayaking, camping, and outdoor events to help prospects visualize ownership.

🔍
High Impact

Segment new subscribers by their primary outdoor activity

Add a preference survey asking whether they're shopping for water sports, camping, stadium events, or home use. Tailor welcome flow messaging to their specific needs—kayakers see back support benefits, campers see portability, stadium-goers see compact convenience.

02

Increase Repurchase of Original Chair

Drive repeat purchases by educating customers on care, positioning the Original Chair as a gift, and reaching out when they're planning their next adventure.

📬
Critical Impact

Deploy seasonal direct mail postcards before peak outdoor seasons

Send physical postcards in March (spring camping prep) and November (holiday gifting) to Original Chair buyers with a "gift your adventure buddy" message. Include a discount code for buying a second chair—position it as the perfect gift for their camping partner, fishing buddy, or fellow kayaker.

🎁
High Impact

Create a post-purchase gifting campaign 60 days after first purchase

Send an email series highlighting the Original Chair as the ultimate outdoor gift with messaging like "Your camping buddy needs one too." Include gift-wrapping options and a multi-chair discount to encourage bulk purchases for family trips or adventure groups.

💡
Medium Impact

Build a post-purchase education flow with care and usage tips

Launch a 4-email series (days 3, 7, 14, 30) teaching customers how to care for their Original Chair, pack it efficiently, and use it across different activities. Include tips for adjusting straps, cleaning, and storage to maximize longevity and satisfaction, reducing buyer's remorse.

03

Increase Cross-Sell to Other Products

Expand customer lifetime value by introducing complementary products like the HotSeat, Longback Chair, and Air Chair based on their Original Chair usage patterns.

🛤️
Critical Impact

Map purchase paths and create activity-based cross-sell flows

Analyze which products Original Chair buyers purchase next. Build automated flows that recommend the HotSeat for winter campers (30 days post-purchase), the Longback Chair for taller users seeking more support (45 days), or the Air Chair for backpackers wanting ultralight options (60 days).

🎒
High Impact

Create complementary product bundles for different adventure types

Develop themed bundles in your campaigns: "Kayaker's Comfort Kit" (Original Chair + PowerLounger), "Basecamp Setup" (Original Chair + HotSeat + Longback), and "Stadium Fan Pack" (2 Original Chairs). Offer bundle discounts to increase AOV and introduce customers to your full product range.

📊
High Impact

Segment campaigns using RFM analysis to target high-value customers

Identify customers who purchased in the last 90 days with high order values and send them early access to new products like the Air Chair or upgraded Longback models. Target frequent buyers with exclusive "adventure gear" collections featuring premium chairs and accessories before promoting to general list.
