01

Increase Conversion of Valentine's Day Bien Tower

Transform holiday browsers into confident buyers by addressing last-minute gifting anxiety and showcasing your three decades of artisan chocolate expertise.

🎯
Critical Impact

Deploy Occasion-Specific Cart Abandonment Flows for Valentine's Orders

With Valentine's Day being time-sensitive, create urgency-driven abandonment emails that highlight your February 14th delivery guarantee and include social proof from past Valentine's recipients. Add a 48-hour countdown timer for guaranteed delivery and showcase the tower's dramatic unboxing experience.

💝
High Impact

Build a Welcome Series That Educates on Your Full Occasion Calendar

Most visitors discover Biens for one holiday, but you serve 12+ occasions. Create a 3-email welcome flow that introduces new subscribers to your Valentine's tower, then educates them on your Easter, Mother's Day, and corporate gifting options with a "Never Miss an Occasion" calendar download.

⭐
High Impact

Integrate Customer Photos and Gift Recipient Testimonials on PDPs

Your $78 Valentine's tower is a premium purchase—buyers need confidence it will impress. Add a dedicated UGC section on product pages showing real customer unboxing photos and testimonials from gift recipients, not just purchasers, to validate the "wow factor" experience.

02

Increase Repurchase of Valentine's Day Bien Tower

Convert one-time Valentine's buyers into repeat customers by helping them become the go-to gifter in their circle and reminding them of upcoming occasions.

📅
Critical Impact

Launch a Post-Purchase Occasion Reminder Flow with Multi-Holiday Touchpoints

Send a 2-week post-Valentine's email asking "How did they love it?" to gather feedback, then trigger a quarterly occasion reminder series highlighting your Easter (April), Mother's Day (May), and Father's Day (June) towers. Include a "Save Their Favorites" tool so buyers can remember which flavors delighted their recipient.

🎁
High Impact

Create a Direct Mail "Gifting Calendar" for High-AOV Valentine's Purchasers

Customers who spent $78+ on Valentine's are premium gifters. Send them a beautifully branded 2024 gifting calendar 4 weeks after purchase featuring all your seasonal towers with exclusive early-bird discount codes for each upcoming holiday, positioning Biens as their year-round gifting solution.

💌
Medium Impact

Implement a 10-Month Win-Back Campaign Targeting January 2025

Valentine's 2024 buyers will think of you again in January 2025. Build a win-back sequence that starts in December with "Remember how they smiled?" messaging and includes an exclusive returning customer offer for the 2025 Valentine's collection plus new tower options they haven't seen.

03

Increase Cross-Sell to Other Products

Expand customer lifetime value by transitioning Valentine's buyers to your broader occasion portfolio and higher-margin corporate gifting programs.

🏢
Critical Impact

Build a B2B Segmentation Path from B2C Valentine's Purchasers

Many individual Valentine's buyers have corporate gifting needs. Add a post-purchase survey asking "Did you order this for personal or business?" and create a dedicated B2B nurture track introducing your corporate program, bulk discounts, and custom branding options for their company events and client gifts.

🎉
High Impact

Design Occasion-Specific Bundle Cross-Sells in Post-Purchase Flows

After Valentine's delivery, send targeted bundle offers based on the next holiday: "Complete the Trifecta" featuring your Luck of the Irish Tower (March) and Easter Tower (April) at a bundled discount. Show customers how to become the ultimate thoughtful gifter across multiple occasions.

📊
High Impact

Analyze Purchase Paths to Create Color/Size-Based Recommendation Flows

Your towers come in different sizes and color themes—track which Valentine's buyers chose red towers vs. pink, small vs. large. Use this data to personalize cross-sell recommendations for future holidays based on their demonstrated size preferences and color aesthetic, increasing relevance of every campaign.

✨ Quick Wins

Additional Retention Levers

Three high-impact operational improvements to strengthen BiensChocolateCenterpieces' customer foundation.

📸

Trust & Community: Launch a Post-Purchase Photo Incentive Program

Send an email 3 days after Valentine's delivery asking customers to share photos of their recipient's reaction in exchange for $10 off their next order. Use this UGC across your site and social to build trust with future buyers who need to see real-world gifting moments.

📱

Communication Channels: Add SMS for Last-Minute Holiday Urgency

Food gifting is inherently last-minute. Build an SMS strategy focused on the 5-day window before major holidays with "Still available for [Holiday] delivery" messages and express shipping options to capture procrastinating gifters who miss your email campaigns.

🎯

Data & Tech: Implement Occasion-Based RFM Segmentation

Traditional RFM doesn't work for seasonal gifting. Create segments based on which holidays customers have purchased for (Valentine's-only vs. multi-occasion buyers) and time campaigns around their demonstrated gifting patterns rather than generic purchase recency windows.
