Critical Impact

Set up abandonment sequences triggered 2 hours after cart exit featuring testimonials about relaxation benefits, a reminder about discreet packaging (important for hemp products), and a limited-time incentive. Integrate Customer Reviews Directly Into Pre-Purchase Email Flows

Pull your strongest product reviews into welcome and abandonment emails, specifically highlighting testimonials about effectiveness for sleep, anxiety relief, and taste. 02

Increase Repurchase of Ultra Chill Δ9 THC and CBD Gummies

Bridge the expectation gap between first purchase and habitual use by guiding customers through optimal consumption patterns and timing their replenishment perfectly.

📅
Critical Impact

Launch a Replenishment Flow Timed to a 21-Day Consumption Window

With 20 gummies per jar at $34.95, most customers using 1 gummy nightly will run out around day 20-25. Trigger a replenishment email on day 18 with the subject "Running low on Ultra Chill?" and a seamless reorder link. Include a reminder about consistent use for best results and offer a small discount for ordering before they run out completely.

💡
High Impact

Create a Post-Purchase Education Series with Usage Tips and Timeline Expectations

Send 3 educational emails over 14 days post-purchase: day 3 (how to find your ideal dose), day 7 (expected effects timeline and when to adjust), day 14 (check-in with usage tips for different wellness goals—sleep vs. stress). This keeps your brand top-of-mind during the trial period and increases confidence, directly combating churn from "didn't work for me" misconceptions.

✉️
Medium Impact

Deploy a 60-Day Win-Back Sequence for Lapsed One-Time Buyers

Target customers who purchased once but haven't reordered in 60+ days with a 3-email win-back series: email 1 asks "What held you back?" with a survey link, email 2 addresses common objections (potency, price, effects), email 3 offers a "welcome back" incentive. Hemp customers often try multiple brands—this recaptures them before they become loyal elsewhere.

Increase Cross-Sell to Other Products

Maximize customer lifetime value by strategically introducing your CBD oils, sleep gummies, and other hemp products based on their Ultra Chill usage patterns and wellness goals.

🎯
High Impact

Build a Post-Purchase Cross-Sell Flow Based on Wellness Goals

30 days after first Ultra Chill purchase, segment customers by likely usage (survey data or purchase behavior) and send targeted recommendations: sleep-focused users get your CBD Sleep Gummies, daytime anxiety users get your CBD Oil Tinctures for more precise dosing, and chronic users get your high-potency Delta-9 options. Include a bundle discount to incentivize multi-product orders.

📦
High Impact

Create Complementary Product Bundles at Strategic Price Points

Promote bundles like "Complete Calm Kit" (Ultra Chill Gummies + CBD Oil Tincture) or "Sleep Stack" (Ultra Chill + CBD Sleep Gummies) at a 15% discount compared to buying separately. Feature these in replenishment emails and on product pages. Bundles increase AOV while introducing customers to your broader product line, turning single-SKU buyers into category buyers.

🔍
Medium Impact

Segment Campaigns by Purchase Frequency and Product Affinity

Use RFM analysis to identify your most engaged Ultra Chill customers (2+ purchases, last order within 45 days) and create VIP segments who receive early access to new product launches like your Delta-8 line or seasonal flavors. These proven buyers are 3x more likely to try new SKUs—leverage that trust to accelerate adoption of your full catalog.
